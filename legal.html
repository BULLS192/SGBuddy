Privacy, terms & feedback

SGBuddy helps people navigate Singapore. Here is what to know about personal data, the limits of our information, using the service and how to reach us.

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · This notice is available in English. Please contact us if you need help understanding it.

Important: SGBuddy is an independent information tool, not a Singapore government service, an immigration authority, or a monitored emergency channel. Always check official government sources for current rules. In an emergency in Singapore, contact emergency services directly.

01 · Data handling

Privacy notice

SGBuddy is an Omnidite project. For questions about this notice, your information, or an access, correction or deletion request, use the Omnidite contact page.

Information the app handles

  • On-device information: chosen language, user mode, preferences, saved places and stops, some trip information, and the optional arrival checklist can be saved in browser storage for convenience and offline use. They can remain on the device after signing out.
  • Accounts and sync: if you sign up or sign in, your email and authentication information are processed through Supabase. Supported preferences and saved items can be synced to a cloud profile when you use account or profile-sync features.
  • Location: if you grant device/browser permission, SGBuddy uses your location to show nearby information and maps. You can refuse or revoke permission. Requests to mapping or data services may disclose information needed to provide those features.
  • Service and feedback data: hosting and connected providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and error logs. If you submit feedback through the Omnidite contact page, the operator and website providers receive the details you choose to provide.

Why and with whom

Information is used to provide the requested features, keep your preferences and saved items, authenticate or sync your account, maintain security and reliability, and respond to enquiries. Depending on what you use, services may include Vercel (hosting), Supabase (accounts/sync), government/open-data services, weather or transport data, and mapping providers such as OneMap or Google Maps. Those services and external websites may have their own privacy policies and may process data outside Singapore.

Controls, retention and security

You can deny location access, clear browser/site storage, or sign out. Signing out does not erase local copies. Clearing site storage can remove offline content, settings and saved items on that device. Cloud account or profile information needs a separate deletion or access request; use the Omnidite contact page for help. Information may be retained as needed for service operation, security, request handling and legal obligations. We use service-provider safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Please do not include passport numbers, identity documents, medical records, passwords or other sensitive details in routine feedback. Privacy practices may change as features and providers change; we will update this notice accordingly.

02 · Safety and information quality

Accuracy disclaimer

Travel rules, arrival forms, customs restrictions, public-health guidance, government notices, transport arrivals, maps, weather, foreign-exchange rates and events can change. Information may be delayed, incomplete, cached for offline use or affected by third-party outages. Editorial summaries are not live official alerts.

For immigration and entry, verify with ICA; for restricted or declarable goods, check Singapore Customs, HSA and CNB. Consult relevant agencies or qualified professionals where needed. The official source, not SGBuddy's summary, is authoritative.

Emergency numbers are provided as a convenience. SGBuddy does not receive or dispatch emergency requests, continuously monitor emergencies, or guarantee phone/SMS connectivity. Use your device's normal emergency calling function and contact appropriate authorities directly.
